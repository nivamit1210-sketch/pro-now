## 3. What the product already has (W7, PRs #9–#11)

- The demo's order: details → what you do in your own words (the matcher
  ticks services) → home and radius → documents → prices → send.
- The server owns the rules:
  - It lists what is missing.
  - It refuses to submit while anything is missing, prices included.
  - Admin approval is per service.
- Account documents: ID, selfie and tax file.
- Per-service licences with numbers. Recommended ones are marked
  "(מומלץ)".
- A criminal-record certificate is never asked for.
- A customer who already registered skips the intro and the character
  (stored on the server).

### B. Business details in step 1 — **S**
- Demo step 2 asks for:

- Nothing becomes "עסק אומת" from this: that badge stays driven by

### C. Documents per trade, from the research — **M**
- The demo derives the documents from `onboardingDocsFor`
  (`lib/types/src/onboarding-docs.ts`), which follows the research report.
  Examples:
  - the AC technician's licence (the 2025 law);
  - work-at-height above 2 m ("when needed");
  - a mobile-garage licence for on-site tyre work;
  - a recovery vehicle plus a tow-driver permit;
  - third-party insurance and trade certificates, both recommended.
- The product reads `service_requirements`, seeded from the older
  catalogue's `requiredCredentials`. **The two lists have not been
  compared.**
- Work:
  1. Map each pilot service → demo docs → requirement codes, as a test
     that fails on any mismatch.
  2. Update the seed data.
  3. Add a "when needed" level beside mandatory/recommended.
  4. Show the "how we check it" line per document.
- This fills in `CLAUDE.md §4` "mandatory credentials per category" with
  **Amit's recorded decision** (18-ROADMAP, 2026-09-29), not a guess.
  Dvir confirms that in D4.

### D. The professional's own prices and lines — **L**
The demo's price step differs from the product's in three ways:
1. **Example prices are filled in** for them to change.
2. One **shared visit fee** covers all their visit-type services, with a
   "מחיר שונה לשירות מסוים" option to split it.
3. List-priced services have **their own price list**: our rows, which
   they can edit or delete, plus "+ עבודה שלא ברשימה".

- The product has one set of fields per `PriceModel`. It has no LIST
  model and no price-line table.
- Work:
  - a `ProfessionalPriceLine` table with a migration;
  - LIST pricing on the server;
  - the customer side showing the chosen professional's lines;
  - the pricing adapter tests (a `CLAUDE.md §6` business-critical area).
- First check how the customer flow prices LIST services today (roadmap,
  2026-09-28, "prices from the list"). This item may split into D1 (the
  join screen) and D2 (customer pricing).
