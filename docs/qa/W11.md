## Fixed before merge (2026-10-01, PR #44)

The browser specs ran for the first time in CI and found three product bugs:

- **Shops were empty against the real catalogue.** Services were grouped by
  the database's department codes (`HOME_REPAIRS`, `AUTO`, …) typed as the
  pilot's (`HOME_URGENT`, `VEHICLE`, …). Now each service is placed by its
  pilot department, shops follow the street's order, and each service shows
  its customer-facing name and description. The unit test uses a real
  database code.
- **A held key barely moved the character.** Movement ran once per key event;
  it is now a held command applied every frame.
- **No way to walk on a phone.** `movementFromPointer` was never wired. A drag
  on the street now walks (offset from the touch-down point, held until lift).

`e2e/world.spec.ts` walks by keyboard and by drag; both specs pass on WebKit
iPhone 15 and Chromium locally. The manual iPhone pass is still pending.
