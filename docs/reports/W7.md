## What was built

- **Joining:**
  - "אני בעל מקצוע" leads to the professional's sign-in, then the
    application, in the demo's order:
    1. details, and how to be addressed (asked, never guessed);
    2. what they do, in their own words, with the matcher marking what fits;
    3. home and radius;
    4. documents: ID, selfie, tax file, and each service's licences with
       numbers, uploaded to private storage;
    5. their own prices;
    6. send.
  - The server lists what is missing and refuses to submit until nothing is.
  - Never a criminal-record certificate.
- **Approval, per service (CLAUDE.md §3):**
  - Admin decisions on the account, each credential with its expiry, and
    each service.
  - A service is approved only after the account, and only when its
    credentials pass the same rule dispatch applies.
  - A refusal needs a reason, and every decision is in `audit_logs`.
  - The screens are W8; the API exists now.
- **Real supply:**
  - Going online and offline, from the web.
  - The position every 20 s while the app is open.
  - A heartbeat sweep takes a silent professional offline. It says to keep
    the app open (iOS cannot track or wake a web page in the background).
- **Offers:**
  - The server's countdown.
  - **The expected earnings from their own price** (they were never shown
    before).
  - Accept through the atomic accept, or pass it on.
- **The job:**
  - the address released on assignment;
  - who opens the door and the code;
  - Waze and Google Maps by coordinates;
  - each step;
  - the quote builder;
  - the end.
